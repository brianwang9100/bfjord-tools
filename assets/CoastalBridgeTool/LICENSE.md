# Public artwork grant and source credits

Brian Wang dedicates the original bridge, rock, foliage and road/tunnel detail geometry, design recipes, original generated materials/details, mathematical water data maps and supplied scene/review images distributed in bfjord-tools to the public under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). This grant applies to the public snapshot and supersedes the private snapshot's prior reservation of rights. The bridge Blender generator's GPL-3.0-or-later license and the independently authored rock, foliage, terrain-mask, road/tunnel-detail and water-map generators' MIT licenses are separate from this artwork grant.

Third-party artwork and the documented prepared derivatives retain their existing CC0 licenses:

- **Concrete034**, ambientCG / Lennart Demes: [source](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034), [license](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/). Approximation material, not a scan. The original 1.1×0.55 m image is resampled and repeated vertically into a 1.1 m square tile; smoothness is inverse roughness and AO is white where none was supplied.
- **Rock030**, ambientCG / Lennart Demes: [source](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rock030), [license](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/). This is a procedural tileable material, not a scan. The original BFjord rock library uses resized color and OpenGL normal images from this source. Its roughness approximation and packed metallic/smoothness map are original derived channels; exact source and delivered hashes are retained in `assets/BFjordTools/Rocks/provenance.json`.
- **Clean Asphalt**, Dimitrios Savva / Poly Haven: [source](https://polyhaven.com/a/clean_asphalt), [license](https://polyhaven.com/license). A 2.1 m physical tile. The prepared mask stores metallic in R, AO in G and inverse roughness in A.
- **Gravel Floor03**, Charlotte Baglioni ([source](https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_floor_03)); **Park Dirt**, Christopher Melani ([source](https://polyhaven.com/a/park_dirt)); **Leafy Grass**, Charlotte Baglioni ([source](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass)): reviewed Poly Haven road/terrain source maps. Exact download hashes are retained in the public source-credit manifest.
- **Rock Moss Set01** and **Shrub03**: [rock source](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_moss_set_01), [shrub source](https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_03), [license](https://polyhaven.com/license). The scoped supplier manifest preserves original author credits and download hashes. Their Unity prefabs, imported models and prepared images are CC0 derivatives.

The Terrain bank uses seven CC0 Poly Haven surfaces. The retained Leafy Grass, Park Dirt and Gravel Floor 03 credits appear above; [Rocky Terrain](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain) supplies the retained 90 m aerial-outcrop material, with its source receipt in the catalog credits. The 0.5 additions are:

- **Forest Floor**, eye-candy.xyz / Poly Haven: [source](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor), [CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license). Prepared as ForestLitter at its approximately 2.14 m physical footprint.
- **Coast Sand Rocks 02**, Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven: [source](https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_rocks_02), [CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license). Prepared as CoastalShingle at its 15 m footprint.
- **Rock Boulder Dry**, photographed by Dimitrios Savva and processed by Rico Cilliers / Poly Haven: [source](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry), [CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license). Prepared as RockFace at its 1.8 m footprint.

`assets/BFjordTools/TerrainDetail/sources.json` and `surface-bank-sources.json` preserve the pinned source file URLs, supplier checksums and SHA-256 values. The MIT `build_surface_bank.py` generator resizes admitted color/OpenGL-normal maps and packs source AO, displacement and inverse roughness into Unity Terrain's mask channels; its prepared derivatives remain CC0. These inputs are independently licensed source assets, not textures extracted from a vendor environment package.

The 0.5 mature oak, silver birch, hollow fallen log and tall meadow grass, including their original woodland atlas and labeled Blender review images, fall under the original CC0 artwork grant above. Their MIT Blender generators do not read third-party images or models. Supplier scan credits apply to the Terrain materials and retained scanned vegetation, not to these original woodland meshes.

The 0.6 B tree meshes and leaf geometry are original CC0 work. Their shared atlas incorporates **Bark Brown 02**, Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven: [source](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02), [CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license). The 1K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness source maps are retained with supplier verification and SHA-256 hashes in `assets/BFjordTools/FoliageDetail/Woodland06/Sources/sources.json`. Oak uses this scan; birch bark and leaf maps remain original procedural art. This source credit applies to the additive B variants, not the unchanged v0.5 A assets. Powered by Poly Haven.

The water normal, foam, river-current, ocean-crest and waterfall maps under `Packages/com.bfjord.tools/Textures/Water` are original periodic mathematical data with no third-party image inputs. Their generators and separate manifests are distributed under `assets/BFjordTools/Water`; the maps are CC0-1.0 and the Python generators are MIT.

The public FBXs may differ byte-for-byte from earlier workshop exports because private source-file paths were removed from structured string metadata. Geometry and numeric/array properties are preserved. Public manifests contain the new file hashes; [EXPORT_PROVENANCE.json](EXPORT_PROVENANCE.json) records the transformation. No provider endorses this project. No paid NatureManufacture assets or vendor source are distributed.
