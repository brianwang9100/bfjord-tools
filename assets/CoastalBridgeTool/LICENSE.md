# Public artwork grant and source credits

Brian Wang dedicates the original bridge geometry, design recipes, original generated materials/details and supplied scene screenshots distributed in bfjord-tools to the public under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). This grant applies to the public snapshot and supersedes the private snapshot's prior reservation of rights. The Blender generator's GPL-3.0-or-later code license is separate from this artwork grant.

Third-party artwork and the documented prepared derivatives retain their existing CC0 licenses:

- **Concrete034**, ambientCG / Lennart Demes: [source](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034), [license](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/). Approximation material, not a scan. The original 1.1×0.55 m image is resampled and repeated vertically into a 1.1 m square tile; smoothness is inverse roughness and AO is white where none was supplied.
- **Rock030**, ambientCG / Lennart Demes: [source](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rock030), [license](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/). Procedural tileable rock material for terrain. It is separate from the scanned rock model's non-tileable UV atlas.
- **Clean Asphalt**, Dimitrios Savva / Poly Haven: [source](https://polyhaven.com/a/clean_asphalt), [license](https://polyhaven.com/license). A 2.1 m physical tile. The prepared mask stores metallic in R, AO in G and inverse roughness in A.
- **Gravel Floor03**, Charlotte Baglioni ([source](https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_floor_03)); **Park Dirt**, Christopher Melani ([source](https://polyhaven.com/a/park_dirt)); **Leafy Grass**, Charlotte Baglioni ([source](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass)): reviewed Poly Haven road/terrain source maps. Exact download hashes are retained in the public source-credit manifest.
- **Rock Moss Set01** and **Shrub03**: [rock source](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_moss_set_01), [shrub source](https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_03), [license](https://polyhaven.com/license). The scoped supplier manifest preserves original author credits and download hashes. Their Unity prefabs, imported models and prepared images are CC0 derivatives.

The public FBXs may differ byte-for-byte from earlier workshop exports because private source-file paths were removed from structured string metadata. Geometry and numeric/array properties are preserved. Public manifests contain the new file hashes; [EXPORT_PROVENANCE.json](EXPORT_PROVENANCE.json) records the transformation. No provider endorses this project. No paid NatureManufacture assets or vendor source are distributed.
